=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters) ===
Ante is a premium session tracker for people who actually want to know how they play — not just whether they're up.

Track live, hand by hand. Start a session for Poker, Blackjack, Baccarat, Roulette, Sports Betting, or a general bankroll, and log every hand as you go. Your live net updates in real time, so you always know exactly where you stand at the table.

Then Ante shows you the truth. Every session rolls into analytics that go far beyond a running total:

• Live P&L and full session history in one clean dashboard
• Per-game performance — see which game is actually paying and which one is quietly bleeding you
• Blackjack strategy grading — every decision checked against basic strategy for your table's rules, with your accuracy and most common mistakes
• Leak detection — Ante flags real patterns in your play, like betting bigger after a loss, tilting after getting bluffed, or your judgment fading late in sessions
• Win rate, streaks, time played, and return on what you actually wagered

No fluff, no hype — just an honest read on your game, in a fast, private, dark-mode app.

Know how you actually play.

Ante is a tracking and analytics tool for informational and entertainment purposes only. It does not facilitate gambling and does not provide gambling or financial advice.
